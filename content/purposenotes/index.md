# PurposeNotes

PurposeNotes is a local-first notes app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Your notes, tasks, goals, and purposes are stored in a database on your device. There are no accounts and no PurposeNotes servers.

## What it does

- **Meaning-first structure.** Connect notes to the purposes and goals they serve with supertags and a graph view.
- **Private by design.** No analytics, no tracking, no data collection. See the [privacy policy](/purposenotes/privacy/).
- **Optional iCloud sync.** Sync across your devices through your own iCloud account. Payloads are end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload.
- **On-device AI.** AI features run on your device. Cloud AI is off by default and asks for confirmation per action; every access is recorded in the on-device AI Access Log.
- **Import and export.** Import from Apple Notes or Obsidian. Export everything as plain Markdown at any time.
