# Notes that know what they are <em>for</em>

PurposeNotes is a local-first notes app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Your notes, tasks, goals, and purposes live in one graph on your device. There are no accounts and no PurposeNotes servers.

## What it does

<div class="cards">
<div class="card career">
<h3><span class="dot"></span> Meaning-first structure</h3>
<p>Connect every note to the purposes and goals it serves. Supertags and a graph view make the link between today's task and the thing you actually care about visible.</p>
</div>
<div class="card healthy">
<h3><span class="dot"></span> Private by design</h3>
<p>No analytics, no tracking, no data collection, no advertising identifiers. Nothing you write is sent to us. See the <a href="/purposenotes/privacy/">privacy policy</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="card graduate">
<h3><span class="dot"></span> Optional iCloud sync</h3>
<p>Sync across your devices through your own iCloud account. Payloads are end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload, so the contents never leave in readable form.</p>
</div>
<div class="card creative">
<h3><span class="dot"></span> On-device AI</h3>
<p>AI features run on your device. Cloud AI is off by default and asks for confirmation per action; every access is recorded in the on-device AI Access Log.</p>
</div>
<div class="card family">
<h3><span class="dot"></span> Import and export</h3>
<p>Import from Apple Notes or Obsidian. Export everything as plain Markdown at any time — your notes are never locked inside the app.</p>
</div>
</div>
