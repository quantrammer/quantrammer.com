# PurposeNotes Support

## Get help

Email us: **support@quantrammer.com**

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

- Your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 26.1)
- The PurposeNotes version (Settings → About PurposeNotes)
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

We never need — and cannot use — your passphrase. Do not include it in any
message.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?

On your device. PurposeNotes has no accounts and no servers of its own. If
you enable sync, encrypted copies also live in your personal iCloud private
database. See the [privacy policy](https://quantrammer.com/purposenotes/privacy).

### I forgot my passphrase. Can you reset it?

If any of your devices is signed in to your Apple ID and unlocked, open
PurposeNotes there and set a new passphrase in Settings — recovery works
through your Apple ID and iCloud Keychain. We hold no copy of your keys, so
if you lose the passphrase **and** access to every signed-in device / your
Apple ID, the vault cannot be recovered — by design.

### How do I sync across devices?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on each device and enable sync in
Settings. Sync payloads are end-to-end encrypted before they leave the
device.

### How do I import from Apple Notes or Obsidian?

Use the importer in the app. Notes, folder structure, ordering, formatting,
and media are brought in from the source app.

### How do I export or back up my notes?

Settings → Export & backup → "Export everything as Markdown" writes a
plaintext Markdown archive of your vault. Keep exports somewhere safe — they
are not encrypted.

### A supertag / popover / graph isn't behaving. What should I try?

1. Update to the latest version of the app.
2. Relaunch the app.
3. Still stuck? Email us the steps to reproduce (see above).

### How do I request a feature?

Email us. Feature requests are read; requests that fit the product's
meaning-first principles are prioritized.
