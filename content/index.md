# quantrammer

Independent software for Apple platforms. Local-first by default: your data stays on your device unless you choose otherwise.

## Apps

### PurposeNotes

A meaning-first notes app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Notes, tasks, goals, and purposes live in one graph, stored on your device, with optional end-to-end encrypted iCloud sync and on-device AI.
