Notes that know what they are for

PurposeNotes is a local-first notes app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Your notes, tasks, goals, and purposes live in one graph on your device. There are no accounts and no PurposeNotes servers.

Get support Read the privacy policy

What it does

Meaning-first structure

Connect every note to the purposes and goals it serves. Supertags and a graph view make the link between today's task and the thing you actually care about visible.

Private by design

No analytics, no tracking, no data collection, no advertising identifiers. Nothing you write is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync across your devices through your own iCloud account. Payloads are end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload, so the contents never leave in readable form.

On-device AI

AI features run on your device. Cloud AI is off by default and asks for confirmation per action; every access is recorded in the on-device AI Access Log.

Import and export

Import from Apple Notes or Obsidian. Export everything as plain Markdown at any time — your notes are never locked inside the app.
